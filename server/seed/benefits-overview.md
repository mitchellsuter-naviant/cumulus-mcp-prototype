# Benefits Overview

What full-time employees are enrolled in, and how to change it. Enrollment
changes outside open enrollment require a qualifying life event.

## Medical — Health PPO 2500

The company covers 85% of the premium. The plan carries a $2,500 individual
deductible and is HSA-eligible; the company contributes $750 to the HSA each
January.

## Dental and vision

Preventive dental is covered at 100% with two cleanings a year. Vision
carries a $200 annual allowance for frames or contacts.

## Retirement — 401(k)

The company matches 100% of your contributions up to **4% of salary**,
vested immediately. Contribution changes take effect the following pay
period; manage them in the payroll portal.

## Learning stipend

$1,200 per calendar year for courses, books, certifications, and
conferences. Spend it by submitting a reimbursement with category
`software` or `supplies` and a note naming the course or event.
