# Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue **20 days of PTO per year** (1.67 days per month),
available as accrued. Unused days carry over up to a cap of 10 days at
year-end.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests through the Employee Hub. The system reads this section as
governed values — editing it changes how requests are handled, and every edit
is logged.

- Requests of 3 working days or fewer auto-approve when the balance covers
  them, and land on your manager's calendar automatically.
- Longer requests route to the HR queue for approval; plan for a one-business-day
  turnaround.
- A request exceeding your accrued balance is declined automatically — split
  it or wait for accrual.

## Blackout and coverage

- Finance close week (first week of each quarter) requires department-head
  sign-off for the finance-ops team.
- Ensure coverage is named in the request note for customer-facing roles.

## Sick time and leave

Sick time is separate and untracked up to 5 consecutive days; longer medical
leave, parental leave, and bereavement follow the leave guide maintained by
the People team.
