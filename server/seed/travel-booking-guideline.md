# Corporate Travel Booking Guideline

Book all business travel through the corporate travel portal.

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer flights.
- Hotel budget: up to $250/night in standard markets, $400/night in high-cost cities.
- Rental cars: midsize or below unless carrying equipment.
- Personal travel extensions are allowed but must be paid personally and noted on the itinerary.

Expense reimbursement for travel follows the Expense Approval Policy.
