# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one
- Provision laptop and accounts (IT ticket, 5 business days lead time).
- Assign an onboarding buddy.

## Week one
- Complete security awareness training.
- Review the expense approval policy and travel booking guideline.
- Meet with manager to set 30/60/90-day goals.

## First month
- Complete role-specific training modules.
- Shadow two customer calls (customer-facing roles).
